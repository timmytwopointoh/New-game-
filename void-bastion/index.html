HOW TO PLAY

1 / Deployment. Player One places at least two units on the blue side. Pass the device. Player Two deploys on the orange side. Each has 14 requisition.

2 / Issue orders. Tap your unit, then an empty tile to move or an enemy in range to attack. Orders stay queued until End Turn; tap a selected unit again to cancel its order.

3 / Execute. End Turn launches animated moves and fire, including automatic turret fire. Then a privacy screen hands control to your rival.

Camera. Drag your finger to orbit the 3D battlefield, pinch or use + / − to zoom, or press ↺ to restore the tactical view. Tap without dragging to choose a tile.

4 / Victory. Destroy the opposing fortress core. Gain 3 requisition every full round; deployment purchases are currently restricted to setup.
