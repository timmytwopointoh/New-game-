# FRACTURED REALMS — Five-level design brief

Core loop: see the landscape -> explore ruined causeway -> collect 3 lost memories -> repel the realm's Stalkers, Wardens and Casters -> defeat its Guardian -> walk through the activated portal -> unlock the next realm. Health is 100, checkpoints currently only at stage start, and Save stores the highest unlocked realm offline.

| Realm | Environment and scene character | Guardian identity | Memory objective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1: Ember Causeway | Black stone, amber furnaces, cliff ruins, arch gate | Ashbound Sentinel | Rekindle three ash memories |
| 2: Hollowglass Forest | Crystalline foliage, bioluminescent trees, teal veins | Glassroot Custodian | Rebind three forest memories |
| 3: Drowned Archive | Submerged vaults, flooded shelves, pale reflected light | Tide Archivist | Recover three lost records |
| 4: Iron Basilica | Mechanical furnaces, plated shrines, molten seams | Iron Cantor | Restore three forge relics |
| 5: Crown of Hours | Floating luminous shards, violet obelisks, lunar gates | Chronarch | Repair the last three time fragments |

**Opening cinematic:** aerial glide through the realm while three lines of story captions appear over a dark letterbox. Reusable per-level entrance. A later milestone should implement storyboarded motion, performance art, layered sound/music and bespoke cinematic assets.

**Visual target:** original dark-fantasy stylization first; increasingly detailed VQ-reviewed modular geometry, proper UVs, baked PBR, sculpted silhouettes, LOD and skeletal deformation in subsequent passes. This design is not a claim of current AAA quality.

**Concrete next quality gates:** scripted playthrough of all five levels; actual Android APK screenshots showing world and HUD; collision/AI tests; 30 FPS goal on midrange hardware; import and approve one VQ GLB with provenance; refine one level to higher visual density before applying it everywhere.
